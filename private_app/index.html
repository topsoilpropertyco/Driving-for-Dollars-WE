Every drive

Three simple steps

  1. Before leaving: open Traccar and turn Continuous tracking on.
  2. Drive normally: lock the phone and use navigation if you want.
  3. After parking: turn Continuous tracking off, then open Five Pointes to see the completed route.

You do not create or copy recorder settings for each drive. The green tracker light means a private report arrived within the last two minutes; it is not a direct reading of the Traccar switch.
